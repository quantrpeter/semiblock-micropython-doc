# How to Add & Name Objects

Learn how to add new characters or items (sprites) to your stage and give them clean names for easy coding!

---

## Step 1 - Open the Object Library
Click the **"Add object"** button in the bottom-right panel as shown in the interface to open the object library.

> ![](img/sk2.1.png){width=inherit}

---

## Step 2 - Choose and Rename Your Character
1. Search or scroll through the library to find the character you want.
2. Click to add it to your stage.
3. In the object settings panel, set its name.

> ![](img/sk2.2.png){width=inherit}

---

> 💡 **Tip for Coding:** Renaming objects to short, clear names (like `Penguin1` instead of `P1`) makes writing your MicroPython code much faster and cleaner!
